Open it in Google Sheets (free)

  1. Go to drive.google.com and sign in.
  2. Click New › File upload and choose Life-Planner-Blank.xlsx.
  3. Double-click the file, then File › Save as Google Sheets. Work in the new Google Sheets copy.

Open it in Microsoft Excel

  1. Double-click Life-Planner-Blank.xlsx (Excel 2016 or newer, Windows or Mac, or Excel for the web).
  2. If a yellow bar says Protected View, click Enable Editing.

Set up in two minutes

  1. Open the Setup tab: type your name and your currency symbol.
  2. Type the first day of the month you are planning, for example 1 Nov 2026.
  3. Rename the income and expense categories to match your life. Every tab picks up the new names.
The one rule: type only in the white cells. Colored cells hold the formulas that make the planner work.

How each tab works

DashboardIncome, expenses, balance, safe-to-spend per day, next tasks, habits, cleaning due, savings and today's meals. Nothing to type.
Money LogThe only place you type money: date, Income or Expense, category, amount. 500 rows.
BudgetType your planned amount per category. Actual, left and percent used are calculated for the Setup month.
AnnualAll 12 months of the Setup year, built from the Money Log.
Savings & Debt12 savings goals and 12 debts. Type the target or balance and what you have saved or paid.
Tasks100 tasks with category, due date, priority and status. Choose Done to cross a task off.
Habits20 habits for the Setup month. Type a lowercase x on each day you did it.
CleaningType the date you last did a chore; next due date and status update.
Goals10 goals with why, deadline, five steps (tick with x) and a reward.
Week PlanType the Monday date (or keep the automatic current week) and plan by the hour.
MealsPlan the week and write your grocery list by aisle.
CalendarThe Setup month with the number of open tasks due each day.

Start a new month

Change the planning month in Setup. Keep logging in the same Money Log; Budget, Habits and Calendar switch to the new month, Annual keeps the whole year. For habits, clear the x marks or keep a copy of last month's tab.
