Your budget in one app, your to-do list in another, habits on paper and meal plans nowhere? This life planner puts all of it in one spreadsheet that updates itself.

Works in Google Sheets (free) and Microsoft Excel. Undated, so you can start any month and reuse it every year.

WHAT YOU GET
• Life-Planner-Blank.xlsx — ready for your own data
• Life-Planner-Example.xlsx — filled with sample data so you can see every tab working
• START HERE guide (PDF, 2 pages) — how to open it in Google Sheets or Excel and set it up

THE 14 TABS
1. Start Here — a one-page tour
2. Dashboard — income, expenses, balance, safe to spend per day, next tasks, habits, cleaning due, savings and today's meals
3. Setup — your name, currency symbol, month and your own budget categories (10 income, 20 expense)
4. Money Log — the only place you type money (500 rows)
5. Monthly Budget — planned vs actual, amount left and percent used per category
6. Annual Overview — all 12 months, built automatically from the Money Log
7. Savings & Debt — 12 savings goals and 12 debts with progress bars
8. Task List — 100 tasks with category, due date, priority, status and days left
9. Habit Tracker — 20 habits x 31 days with total, month score and streak
10. Cleaning Schedule — daily to yearly chores; next due date and Overdue status are calculated
11. Goal Tracker — 10 goals, each with why, deadline, 5 steps and a reward
12. Weekly Planner — your week hour by hour, 6:00 to 22:00
13. Meal Planner & Grocery List — breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks, plus a shopping list by aisle
14. Monthly Calendar — the number of open tasks due on each day

WHY YOU'LL LIKE IT
✓ Type once: one row in the Money Log updates your budget, annual view and dashboard
✓ White cells for typing, colored cells for formulas, so nothing breaks
✓ Rename any category and every tab follows
✓ Any currency: type your symbol in Setup

HOW IT WORKS
1. Download the files after purchase.
2. Excel: open the .xlsx. Google Sheets: upload it to Google Drive, then File > Save as Google Sheets.
3. Fill in Setup (2 minutes) and start logging.

WHAT YOU NEED
Google Sheets (free Google account) or Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer, including Excel for the web. Apple Numbers is not supported.
